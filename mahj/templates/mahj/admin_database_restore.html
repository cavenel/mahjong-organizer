Snapshot restore

Roll the whole local database back to one of the automatic snapshots taken while the app runs. The restore is applied the next time you launch the app.

Restoring overwrites the whole local database. Everything entered since the chosen snapshot is lost — a restore replaces, it never merges.

To bring back a single tournament instead (from this or another install), use Backup & restore.
