Scanning

Photo scanning reads a paper score sheet into the app. You need to set up two things: an API key to pay for the reading, and a picture of your score sheet. Scanning stays off until both are set.

Scanning is optional.

You do not have to set this up. Scorers can enter every score sheet by hand, which is what the app does by default. Scanning only saves typing: a player photographs the paper sheet, and a scorer checks the result. Nothing else in the app depends on it.

API key

Scanning uses the Anthropic API. It is billed to your own account. Reading one photo costs about a cent. A 16-table, 9-round tournament is roughly 150 photos. Create a key on your Anthropic API keys page.

Before you paste a key

  • Anyone with the link to the scan page can spend this key. The app allows 6 photos a minute per device. That slows a burst down, it does not cap the total.
  • Use a separate key for this, and set a spend limit on it in the Anthropic console. You can then delete the key after the event.

What the box has to contain

All 16 hands, in three columns. Your sheet can be laid out differently from the example, but the columns have to mean this:

Value
The points for the hand. If nobody won the hand, leave it blank, or put a 0 in the Winner column.
Winner
The winner's seat at the table, 1 to 4. Not a name, not a wind letter, and not the player number from the draw.
Discarder
Who discarded, also as a seat 1 to 4. On a self-draw, leave it blank, or repeat the winner's number. Both are read as a self-draw, so use whichever your tables already use.

Anything the reader cannot make out is left as an empty cell for a scorer to fill in. One unreadable digit does not stop the rest of the sheet.

A flat scan or a straight-on photo of an empty sheet. Up to 4 MB.

Drag a box around the score columns (Value, Winner, Discarder for all 16 hands). Only that part of the sheet is read. Leave a small margin around the columns. Handwriting often goes outside the ruled cells, and the matching is a few pixels off, so a tight box can cut the edge of a digit.

Rehearse a scan

Upload your sheet above first. Then photograph a filled-in sheet the way a player would. Nothing here writes to a score sheet.

  • Test alignment shows you the exact strip the reader sees. It is free, because no sheet is read. Use it until the box is right.
  • Test scan does a real read, billed to your key, about a cent. This is the only check that tests everything. The strip can look correct and still be read wrong if your columns do not match. Do this once before the event.
